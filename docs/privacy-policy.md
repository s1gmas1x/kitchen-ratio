# Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 20, 2026

KitchenRatio ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and your choices.

## Information We Collect

- Usage data:
  We use Google Analytics to understand general site usage, such as page views, approximate location, device type, and referrer data.
- Tip checkout data:
  If you choose to leave a tip, checkout is handled by a third-party payment processor. We do not store your full payment card details on KitchenRatio.
- Contact data:
  If you contact us directly, we may receive the information you include in your message.

## How We Use Information

- To operate and improve KitchenRatio.
- To measure content performance and site reliability.
- To process optional tip payments through our payment provider.
- To detect abuse and maintain service security.

## Cookies and Tracking

We use a consent banner for analytics cookies. Analytics tracking is enabled only after you accept. You can change your preference using the "Cookie Preferences" control in the footer.

See `/cookie-policy` for more details.

## Sharing

We may share limited data with service providers that help operate the site, such as analytics and payment providers. We do not sell personal information.

## Data Retention

We retain data only as long as needed for the purposes described above, unless a longer period is required by law.

## Your Choices

- Decline analytics cookies from the banner.
- Reopen cookie settings from the footer.
- Use browser controls to clear stored cookies.

## Children

KitchenRatio is not intended for children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy over time. The "Last updated" date reflects the current version.

## Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent at `ckohl.com/contact`.
