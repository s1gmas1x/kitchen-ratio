# Getting Started With KitchenRatio

KitchenRatio helps you build and scale dough formulas quickly using baker’s percentages.  
If you want the shortest path: pick a preset, set flour, and go to recipe.

## 1. Pick A Preset
Start by choosing a preset (bread, pizza, flatbread, etc.).  
Presets load proven starting values, and in many cases you can go straight to the recipe from there.

After selecting a preset, use the **Instructions** button to open a matching recipe guide.

## 2. Set Flour Weight
Use the `+` and `-` buttons to scale your batch size.

- More flour = more dough
- Less flour = less dough

This can be as small as a single tortilla or large enough for many pizzas.  
All other ingredient amounts update automatically to keep baker’s percentages consistent.

You can also open **Flour Options** to create flour blends.

## 3. Adjust Main Ratios
Tune the core formula:

- Hydration
- Salt
- Yeast

You can also set water temperature.  
If you choose **active dry yeast**, extra options appear for bloom liquid source and bloom settings.

## 4. Add Ingredients
Add ingredients from categories like:

- Fats
- Sugars
- Dairy
- Seeds/Nuts
- Starches
- Misc

Use these sliders (plus the main sliders above) to fine-tune the dough:

- Increase hydration
- Add a little more egg
- Add ingredients
- Swap/substitute ingredients

When you choose a preset, the ingredient sliders that appear are pulled from this same ingredient list.

## 5. Check Dough Stats
The Dough Stats section helps with planning and consistency:

- **Total dough estimate** for batch sizing
- **Yield Planner** to estimate pieces (like rolls or tortillas)
- **Hydration (liquids)** based on added liquids (water, milk, coffee, etc.)
- **Effective Hydration** including water contributed by ingredients like eggs, yogurt, and butter
- **Absorption estimate** to account for thirstier ingredients (like rye or potato)

Use this section to build better intuition for dough feel and repeatable results.

## 6. Review Recipe
The Recipe section reflects your current slider settings.  
It also correctly accounts for flour/liquid allocated to:

- Preferments
- Scalds
- Yeast bloom liquid

Use **Share Recipe** to copy a URL with your current settings.  
You can share that link or paste it somewhere to save your formula.
