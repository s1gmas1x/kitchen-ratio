# Cookie Policy

Last updated: February 20, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how KitchenRatio uses cookies and similar technologies.

## What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They help sites remember settings and understand usage.

## Cookies We Use

- Consent cookie:
  `ga-consent` stores whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies.
- Analytics cookies:
  If you accept, Google Analytics may set cookies to measure site traffic and engagement.

## Why We Use Cookies

- Remember your cookie preference.
- Understand aggregate site usage so we can improve content and performance.

## Managing Cookies

- Use the banner when it appears to accept or decline.
- Use the "Cookie Preferences" button in the footer to reopen choices.
- You can also manage or delete cookies in your browser settings.

## Third-Party Cookies

When analytics is enabled, Google may place cookies according to its own policies.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this page as our cookie usage changes. The "Last updated" date reflects the current version.
