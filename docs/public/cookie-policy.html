Cookie Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how KitchenRatio uses cookies and similar technologies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to remember preferences and help improve site experience.

Cookies and storage we use

Name Type Purpose Duration
ga-consent Cookie Stores your analytics consent choice (accepted or declined). Up to 12 months
kitchenratio:recipe:v1 Local storage Saves calculator settings and preferences in your browser. Until cleared
theme Local storage Saves light/dark theme preference. Until cleared
kitchenratio:pwa-install-toast-last-at Local storage Stores when the install suggestion toast was last shown to reduce repeat prompts. 7 days (rolling cooldown)

Analytics

Analytics scripts are loaded only after consent is accepted. If you decline, analytics tracking is not enabled. A consented Amazon affiliate-link click records only the stable product key and the current KitchenRatio page path; it does not block or delay navigation.

Third-party cookies and services

When analytics is enabled, analytics providers may place cookies according to their own policies. If you choose to leave a tip, checkout is handled by a third-party payment processor that may use cookies or similar technologies needed to process payment and prevent fraud.

Managing cookies and storage

  • You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings at any time.
  • You can block cookies in your browser, though some features may not work as intended.
  • You can reopen cookie choices from the footer using "Cookie Preferences".
