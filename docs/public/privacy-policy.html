Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 12, 2026

KitchenRatio respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Calculator settings stored locally: recipe settings, selected ingredients, slider values, preset choice, theme, and related UI preferences are stored in your browser local storage.
  • PWA install prompt preference stored locally: if an app install suggestion is shown or dismissed, we store a local timestamp to reduce repeat prompts (currently a 7-day cooldown).
  • Cookie consent preference: whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies.
  • Analytics data (if consented): standard aggregated usage data such as pages visited and basic device/browser metadata, including install prompt and install events for the web app.
  • Affiliate-link click data (if consented): when you follow an Amazon affiliate link, analytics records the stable product key and the KitchenRatio page path. The event does not include the destination URL or visible link text and does not tell us whether you purchase anything.
  • Tip checkout data: if you choose to leave a tip, checkout is handled by a third-party payment processor. We do not store your full payment card details on KitchenRatio.

How we use information

  • To keep your calculator inputs and preferences between visits.
  • To avoid repeatedly showing app install suggestions in a short period.
  • To improve site performance, usability, and content.
  • To understand aggregate usage trends when analytics consent is granted.
  • To process optional tip payments through our payment provider.

Cookies and analytics

KitchenRatio only enables non-essential analytics after you accept cookie consent. You can change your preference using the "Cookie Preferences" control in the footer. See the Cookie Policy for details.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Aggregated analytics may be processed by analytics providers acting as service providers. We may also share limited data with payment providers to process optional tips.

Following an Amazon affiliate link takes you to Amazon, where Amazon handles the visit under its own policies. Amazon Associates reports attributed orders and earnings by tracking ID separately from KitchenRatio's consented click analytics; KitchenRatio does not combine those reports into individual user profiles.

Your choices

  • You can clear browser local storage to remove saved calculator settings.
  • You can clear cookies to remove consent preferences and analytics cookies.
  • You can decline analytics cookies when prompted.
  • You can reopen cookie settings from the footer using "Cookie Preferences".
